# Q6 full-dev OOF — inharmonicity channel PASSES the gate

Accuracy-loop iteration 12 (ROI deep-dive §4.1). The honest version of the
pilot: **all 300 GuitarSet development clips (players 00-04)**, configuration
**frozen in source before the run** — weight 1.0, `min_r2` 0.50, raw
specification-derived table, no offset, no sweep, one candidate against
baseline. The pilot's +0.0525 was chosen on the same 20 clips it reported;
this removes that optimism.

## Result — PASS

| metric | baseline | arm | Δ [lo-95, hi-95] |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Tab F1 (aggregate)** | 0.6031 | 0.6474 | **+0.0443 [+0.0339, +0.0555]** |
| Tab F1 (solo) | — | — | **+0.0860 [+0.0673, +0.1055]** |
| Tab F1 (comp) | — | — | +0.0026 [−0.0000, +0.0069] |
| onset F1 | 0.9182 | 0.9182 | bit-identical |
| pitch F1 | 0.8951 | 0.8951 | bit-identical |

**Gate = lower-95 CI > 0: PASS**, and not marginally — the lower bound is
**+0.0339**, four bootstrap-CI widths clear of zero. Onset and pitch F1 are
bit-identical to eight decimals, as they must be for a channel that rewrites
string assignment only.

The full-dev figure (+0.0443) sits slightly below the pilot's +0.0525, which
is the expected direction: the pilot's weight was tuned on its own 20 clips,
this run's was not. Landing within ~0.008 of it after removing that bias is
the confirmation the pilot was hoping for.

## The decomposition is a one-for-one conversion at scale

| bucket | baseline | arm | Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| correct | 30,708 | 31,956 | **+1,248** |
| wrong_position_same_pitch | 12,907 | 11,659 | **−1,248** |
| pitch_off | 4,129 | 4,129 | 0 |
| timing_only | 1,032 | 1,032 | 0 |
| missed_onset | 4,985 | 4,985 | 0 |
| extra_detection | 3,797 | 3,797 | 0 |

**Exactly 1,248 wrong-position errors become correct notes; the other four
buckets do not move by a single event across 52,741 detections.** This is the
§6.3 leakage check passing as strictly as it can — the gain is entirely in the
bucket the channel targets, and nowhere else. A physical string-assignment
cue cannot touch detection, and at 300-clip scale it verifiably does not.

## Coverage and per-clip behaviour

| stage | count | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| detected events | 52,741 | 100% |
| isolated | 9,927 | 18.8% |
| fit succeeds (r² ≥ 0.50) | 4,407 | 8.4% |
| evidence applied | 4,354 | 8.3% |

- **129 clips improved, 146 unchanged, 25 regressed.** The 25 regressions are
  the honest cost of a soft evidence term: a note the channel is confident
  about but wrong on can flip a previously-correct assignment. They are
  outnumbered ~5:1 and the aggregate paired CI absorbs them comfortably, but
  they are real and worth stating — this is +0.0443 *on average*, not a
  strict Pareto improvement.
- The lift is **overwhelmingly solo** (+0.0860) with comp barely moving
  (+0.0026, lower bound touching zero). Expected and by design: strummed
  material rarely presents an isolated note, so the channel abstains there.
  Aggregate Tab F1 on a strum-heavy recording will move far less than on a
  fingerstyle one.

## Reproduce

```

python scripts/eval/q6_full_dev.py \
  --json ../docs/EVAL_REPORTS/q6_full_dev_2026-07-22.json
```

Config is frozen in the script (`WEIGHT`, `MIN_R2`); ensemble events cache to
`$TABVISION_DATA_ROOT/models/q6_full_dev_cache/`, so a re-run is minutes.

reused).
